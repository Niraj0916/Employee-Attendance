### Inspected and Decided Was NOT a Defect

1. **Using PyMongo instead of Motor**:
   The starter imported synchronous `pymongo.MongoClient`. Candidate kit specifications explicitly state: *"Stack: Python 3.11+, FastAPI, PyMongo or Motor"*. Because FastAPI runs synchronous endpoint handlers in an optimized threadpool (`anyio`), PyMongo is fully thread-safe and avoids asynchronous driver edge cases. We confirmed that PyMongo is appropriate and fulfills all concurrency requirements.
2. **Addressing attendance records by natural key `(emp_code, date)` without synthetic ID**:
   The starter did not have an independent attendance record ID in its query paths. Section 5 of `PROBLEM_STATEMENT.docx` confirms: *"Attendance records have no id either; a record is addressed by (emp_code, date)"*. This is deliberate domain design, not a defect.
3. **Omitting `history` array in seeded legacy records**:
   Legacy documents in `sample_data/` omit `history` or `half_day`. Section 5 notes: *"Seeded records may omit history or half_day; treat them as empty and false"*. This was verified and properly handled by defaulting missing fields during query and serialization.
